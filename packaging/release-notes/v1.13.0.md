jekyll-vitepress-theme 1.13.0 serves Inter from your own site and preloads it, so your pages no longer jump when the font arrives, and they make no request to Google.

```sh
bundle update jekyll-vitepress-theme
```

## Changed

* **Inter comes with the theme.** Pages loaded Inter from Google Fonts after the first paint, and the text re-wrapped when it swapped in: on zapfast.rocks that cost 0.09 to 0.19 of Cumulative Layout Shift, the score search engines use for pages that move while loading. Inter 4 now ships in the gem with its SIL Open Font License, its Latin face is preloaded, and Lighthouse measures no layout shift on those pages. Visitors' browsers no longer contact Google for it. Sites that set `typography.google_fonts_url` keep loading that stylesheet from Google, and `false` still loads no web font. By @crmne
